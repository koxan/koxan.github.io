Privacy Policy

Captain Studio · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Captain Studio (“the app”) is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “Captain Bayraktar” to publish the owner’s own videos. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google) account of the channel owner, through Google OAuth, with the scopes needed to upload videos and to read the owner’s own channel and video statistics (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).
  • Video files, titles and descriptions that the channel owner prepares and approves for publishing.

The app does not access, collect or store data of any other Google users.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos approved by the channel owner to the owner’s own YouTube channel at scheduled times.
  • To read view statistics of the owner’s own videos so the owner can plan future content.

Captain Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens and video files are stored only on the channel owner’s private server.
  • Data is never sold, never shared with third parties and never used for advertising.
  • Video files are deleted from the server after publishing or after a short retention period.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access the account.
