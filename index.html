Captain Studio

Private publishing tool of the YouTube channel “Captain Bayraktar”.

Captain Studio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Captain Bayraktar channel. It schedules and uploads short videos that the channel owner has prepared and approved to the owner’s own YouTube channel (and to the owner’s own Facebook and Instagram pages), and reads basic view statistics of the owner’s own videos to plan future content.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other person’s account.
